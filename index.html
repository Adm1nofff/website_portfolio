My Story

Overall, my story began during my teenage years, or maybe even earlier, when I spent a lot of time at the computer and was truly inspired by people who were able to present their own interesting projects to the world — projects that could change entire industries, especially the gaming industry, because for many of us, it all started with games.

My first game was Half-Life. It was a truly great gaming project, and in my opinion, it inspired many people to begin their journey in programming. The same thing happened to me.

Later in life, I realized that my path in programming should truly begin at university. At the age of 17, I entered Antalya Bilim University. Far away from my home, in a foreign country, I touched the world of engineering for the first time.

Of course, during my university years, I studied "very hard" :) But that did not stop me from learning the fundamentals, which I later used to create university projects and which I will continue to develop in the future.

In the middle of my studies, I realized that I was more drawn not to monotonous and uninspired code writing, but to creative thinking, generating ideas, and bringing them to life.
